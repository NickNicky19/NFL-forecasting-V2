## What changed when coverage expanded

On the 167 qualified outcomes among the 177 originally unselected records, saved adjusted-point direction is **89/167 (53.29%)**, raw-field direction **83/167 (49.70%)**, and always-Under **97/167 (58.08%)**. The original rule abstains on all 177. The higher original selected-pool rate therefore cannot be applied to the full pool, and the full-pool drop is not a pure learner effect. Coverage, source composition and selection policy differ.

On the identical 446 full-pool outcomes, the adjustment wins eleven more directions than the saved raw field: 272 versus 261. The [paired comparison annotations](COMPARISON_ANNOTATIONS.json) retain same-case error differences and game-cluster sensitivity. Each source point was already rounded to two decimals; strict internal-unrounded P1 is unavailable. The `raw_projection` label identifies a retained field and its exact archived `provider_point` parity, not authenticated external supplier training or vintage.
