It reports CURRENT versus RECENT and RECENT versus MULTI for all completed supported V2 procedures across2022–2026. These are new paired analyses of retained predictions, not new forecasts, fits or independent NFL observations. Mean MSE is separate from native point MAE and TD Brier. Existing proper-law score analyses remain separate.

MULTI improves RECENT in8 targets and loses on interceptions, receptions, combined yards and chart PPR. In2025 RECENT improves11/12 and MULTI improves10/12; in2024 RECENT improves12/12 and MULTI improves6/12. These are one fixed recipe, not evidence every procedure gains or a selected future routing policy.
