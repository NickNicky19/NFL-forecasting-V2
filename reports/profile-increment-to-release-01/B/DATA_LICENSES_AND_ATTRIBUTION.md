# Data attribution and publication scope

New passing-opportunity source derivatives and model inputs use retained nflverse/nflfastR play-by-play and official statistical derivatives under their existing CC BY4.0 contract. Cite nflverse/nflfastR and the source/version hashes in source-profiles/SOURCE_COVERAGE_V2.json. Current-vintage reconstruction does not authenticate original publication time.

Existing F2/depth-catchability control comparisons reuse their prior FTN Data/nflverse CC BY-SA4.0 bindings. No new FTN source was collected or re-fit in this wave. Those source/annotation and licensing limits remain in their original packages.

New typed models and profiles derive solely from the permitted public football inputs. Private offered lines/rows remain owner-local. Public scores, safe commitments and code do not grant access to restricted A/B archives or authenticate quote execution. No private source locators, credentials, raw proprietary offer rows or protected legacy models are included.
