## Workload and similar-player mechanics

R0 has no explicit local attempts-to-completions or carries-to-yards model. `player_props` starts with Sleeper/RotoWire's category projection. With no projection, it can fall back to a current-season arithmetic mean after at least two played games. The provider's internal training years, injury treatment and workload equations are opaque here. Locally, the point is multiplied by a same-position opponent factor: contributors must have at least two prior-current-season games, and their average outside the game against this defense must be between half and twice the target's current-season category average. With no target average, the category's minimum pickable line supplies a lower boundary. Pool actual production against the defense over contributors, divide by pooled expected production, shrink toward one with n/(n+8), and clamp to [0.75,1.25]. There is no nearest-neighbor learned distance, explicit exposure normalization, or causal defensive-talent interpretation in this formula.

At a current forecast origin, all contributor games are earlier than the target week. A contributor's baseline for a prior game may use other games occurring after that prior game but before the current query; that is available context for the current query, and is not by itself leakage. Historical replay still requires that query-specific cutoff and correct roster/source vintage. R0's display defense ranks and factor are different quantities. Do not infer that a strong displayed rank improves the forecast.

R0's last-four-played-game snap share is the mean of available off_snp/tm_off_snp ratios; missing ratios are omitted. It gates Top Picks at 50% and does not estimate the point's exposure. The source has no retained nonmissing-denominator count in that field. Snaps are not routes, and source rights do not transfer automatically to V2.

The research base feature code creates last-three/eight slot means with finite observation counts, targets, matched-exposure pooled rates, prior team pass/rush production, raw opponent allowed totals, and earlier-opponent adjusted pass/rush yard residuals. Unknown participation consumes a slot. Permitted-source qualification requires a positive official physical event to establish played, and masks unsupported labels; this is insufficient to prove complete played-zero/DNP populations. Ordered-recent v1 adds four exact count-history slots plus age/state masks for carries and receptions/targets only. Construction is verified, but proving a shortlisted learner consumes a column requires its exact resolved estimator feature list and artifact. These are not available solely from a field name or current P2_SELECTED wrapper.

## Defense, playcalling and timing

Research base adjusted ratings subtract a prior opponent expectation pooled over eight earlier games with four league-prior games, then average residuals and add the current earlier-only league mean. This is stronger context than final-season ranks, but no no-defense/current-defense/richer-feature fixed-learner ablation was executed by this audit. That comparison remains necessary for incremental-value claims. R0 has no local pressure/coverage/personnel group. The base research group contains a pass-attempt/(pass-attempt+carry) proxy, not actual snap or clock pace; it explicitly omits authenticated historical news, injuries, routes, forecast weather and private schemes. No access to unreleased playbooks is claimed.

The seven-day assumption is explicit in features.py and operational-source-policy-v2. The operational policy admits a whole season/week only when every scheduled regular-season game's kickoff+7d is earlier than asof. That can remove even more recent games than a per-game rule. It is not proof of target leakage or proof that the real public feed was unavailable sooner. A repair must obtain source-time evidence, version the policy, rebuild features and compare under unchanged methods/populations. Replacing the assumption with a shorter lag without historical availability proof would not establish an as-of reconstruction.
