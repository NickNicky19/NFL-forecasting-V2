## Required semantic checks for the B implementation

1. The pinned PBP dictionary says `pass_attempt` **includes sacks**. Define official attempts and throw-eligible denominators explicitly, including sacks, no-plays, two-point plays and exceptional attribution; reconcile with the supported official player-game target. `qb_dropback` is a different exposure. Do not assume a field name proves target parity.
2. Preserve negative/missing `air_yards`. A depth-share denominator counts only eligible throws with known depth and must be accompanied by unknown-depth coverage. Do not convert missing depth to a zero-yard screen. Down/distance/type mixes are earlier-game inputs or predicted future mixes, never target-game realized predictors.
3. FTN `is_catchable_ball` means generally on-target throws **not defended away**; it is not an isolated causal QB-accuracy measure. Join by exact nflverse game plus play ID, enforce cardinality, and retain uncharted/unknown flags. A missing flag is not false. `read_thrown` primary-read coding is unavailable in 2022, according to the dictionary.
4. Compare a strong unchanged direct baseline against one registered context addition, then personalization/shrinkage separately. A historical completion fraction is not an authenticated catchability label. A conditional conversion law may train on observed historical exposure, but inference must use predicted exposure and propagate its uncertainty. Direct models should not be assigned invented opportunity components.
5. Fit bins, imputation, scale, shrinkage and any player/peer effects earlier-only. Preserve Week 1, unseen-QB and sparse-role fallbacks. Historical participation-only membership remains retrospective; it does not recreate the ex-ante QB or actual-offer population. A new public-data source does not reset prior research exposure.
