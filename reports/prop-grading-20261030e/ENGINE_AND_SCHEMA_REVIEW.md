The source contract must establish what a zero means and whether a forecast is conditional on appearance. A missing field is not zero, and a source numeric zero alone does not prove participation. Early exits remain under the actual bookmaker rules. Full-game results include overtime when that is the registered product. Separate scalar forecast error, statistical line crossing, three-way Over/Under/push outcomes, selected-side hit rate, probability quality and price-aware return.

For reporting, selected hit rate is wins divided by wins plus losses, with pushes, voids, pending and unresolved states counted separately. An all-refund pool has null decisive hit rate. Per-selected, per-resolved-stake and per-offered-opportunity return denominators must be named rather than conflated. Missing prices specifically block priced returns, not authenticated direction results. Integer-line book probabilities may be conditional on a decisive result; they cannot be mixed directly with unconditional football probabilities without the supported push/void bridge. Same-book de-vigging requires the same line, time and contract.
