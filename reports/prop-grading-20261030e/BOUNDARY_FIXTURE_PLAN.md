The existing `nfl_v2/scoring.py` remains the scoring engine. This independent plan supplies acceptance boundaries for its exact prop extension; it is not a second production grader. The latest owner brief requires eight settlement states, exact target-aware arithmetic, explicit participation and contract evidence, no assumed prices, no probabilities inferred from points, and separate fixed-week and rolling selections. No NFL outcomes, models or offer rows were decoded for this plan.

Every fixture must identify its layer: statistical crossing, bookmaker settlement, probability, price/EV, source join, or selection. A known line and side can support a statistical crossing without an authenticated bookmaker contract or price. Book settlement requires the actual action/void/push rules; price-aware returns additionally require a real compatible price. An unpriced WIN is not an invented unit profit.

The settlement fixture matrix must exercise **WIN, LOSS, PUSH, VOID, PENDING, MISSING_ACTUAL, UNKNOWN_PARTICIPATION and UNRESOLVED_CONTRACT** separately. Finality, participation, complete-source zeros and contract knowledge are independent inputs. The protocol must register precedence for simultaneous gaps. A DNP, early exit, cancellation, played zero and absent statistic must not be inferred from one another. Partial game totals cannot settle a full-game product. Signed yards and signed/fractional chart points remain valid; natural counts retain integer nonnegative support. A generic fantasy score cannot settle Nick's chart contract without proven parity.

Exact chart comparisons must use sixteen exact components or a source-certified 0.02 grid. They must not round a genuinely off-grid mean, line or recorded provider value. The legacy precision counterexample uses passing yards 0, rushing yards −5 and receiving yards 3, with all other components zero: the exact score is −0.20. The legacy float score −0.19999999999999996 grades as an Over win at −0.20. The correct exact-component result is a push. Historical score bytes remain retained, and a scoring correction earns no predictive-improvement claim.

The [machine-readable fixture matrix](BOUNDARY_FIXTURE_PLAN.json) specifies exact decimal strings and independent expected results. It covers half-point counts and yards, integer pushes, fractional/signed scores, bad support, played zero/DNP, contract and finality gaps, mirrored scores, line monotonicity, push/void mass, conditional versus unconditional EV, duplicate/revised offers and source cutoffs. It also covers P1 unrounded point direction with equality abstention, P2 higher conditional win probability, and P3 highest positive EV at the side's own price. Ties, missing inputs and zero decisive mass require explicit abstention reasons.

Fixed-week top-6/10/20 candidates, scores, prices and tie rules must be frozen before that week's first kickoff. Later genuine pregame information belongs to an explicitly versioned rolling batch. A rolling batch cannot use future quotes, refresh already settled choices or backfill a selected missing result after observing outcomes. The two systems have separate selection IDs, candidate denominators and coverage. Top-player membership and top-pick selection remain different populations. Repeated lines, sides, books and forecasts do not create independent NFL games.

After the parent's protocol and panel are sealed, independent numerical review will verify the retained bytes and exact identity joins, then recompute a win, loss, integer push, void and every supported state from source facts. It will check all aggregate state counts, selection masks, price availability, correct-side denominators, probability conditioning and flat-unit ledger identities. All null outcomes stay visible. Drawdown uses real chronological order. Bootstrap blocks preserve weeks/games and do not turn repeated offers into extra observations. No missing price blocks an otherwise supported direction diagnostic, and no missing odds is filled with −110.

The current univariate result adapter treats only `anytime_td` as a probability target and defaults other categories to point MAE. New threshold probabilities and decision records therefore need an explicitly versioned adapter or supplemental index with the correct product, event denominator, contract, side/line, selection, evidence and metric definition. They must not be fed through the numeric-target MAE/tolerance path and mislabeled as numeric forecast accuracy. Software fixtures remain separate from NFL evaluation counts. Default canonical score-version selection and all frozen past indexes remain unchanged.
