Week5 adds fourteen newly qualified games. Weeks6 and7 reuse frozen grades. Cumulative means only these42 of43 scheduled games in Weeks5–7. It does not cover Weeks1–4 or the Week5 Thursday game. This is statistical crossing with conditional-on-verified-appearance labels; bookmaker settlement, prices, probabilities and returns remain unestablished. No existing forecast or grade was changed.

The comparison universe is exactly the source records with a saved original Over or Under before outcomes. Every control sees that same opportunity set, retains its own chosen side, and preserves abstentions and unknowns. Thus a difference cannot be explained by excluding the original unselected board. Earlier source-vintage uncertainty remains. The full-provider-pool cumulative results are a separate panel.
