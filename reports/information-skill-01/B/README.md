## What changed

The nearest P2/C2 procedures already predict conditional down/depth conversion, use pooled/player/team/opponent evidence and lagged catchability, and integrate forecast attempts. M2 changed variance while preserving means/mix. This test changes a distinct representation and relationship: team-versus-QB throw-choice preferences and opponent context-choice residuals after accounting for each earlier offense’s expected mix. No new external observations or personnel source were obtained. [Prior-use/source feasibility](NEW_INFORMATION_FEASIBILITY_V1.json).

- **C0:** unchanged C2 conditional conversion, static shrunk QB mixture, fixed COMMON_EXPOSURE attempt law. C2 is a strong tied conditional-law parent, not the overall category leader.
- **C1:** the same conversion learner plus two earlier context-choice log ratios; static mix retained.
- **R0:** original conversion and information, with one regularized multinomial mixer.
- **R1:** augmented conversion plus that same mixer with the enriched block.

Team/opponent composition includes credited QB attempts from the canonical QB rows; non-QB passers are excluded. Team profiles use the last16 admitted team games, with20 prior attempts. Opponent profiles compare actual context attempts with expected attempts bound at each prior game’s own cutoff, with100 prior attempts. Counts, denominators, prior mass, raw/shrunk states, source ages and games are retained in the [feature trace](wave-v1/QUERY_FEATURE_TRACE_V1.json.gz). These are observational composition effects, not pure causal defensive strength. The learned R0 slope in W1 was −0.8075 on the QB-versus-league contrast, so its benefit is primarily a learned pooling/reweighting effect. It is not evidence of personnel information.

No receiver-group universe, routes/alignment, pressure, weather, injury statuses or target-game play mix was supplied. Future context counts are multinomial conditional on predicted attempts; their conversion is integrated analytically. The attempt law is identical across all arms. This does not model posterior parameter uncertainty or correlated game-level play calls.
