# Shared player/workload cohorts: retained statistical directions

These aggregate all ten supported targets in the one thirteen-game Week 7 source panel. Player ranks are target-specific; they are not top-ranked picks. Membership is joined from the original 1,973 W7 cohort records by canonical game/player/target/population: 446 supported rows match, including all 167 qualified formerly unselected records. Sixteen unknown appearances remain an explicit unknown-membership group. No rank or workload membership is inferred from target-game performance. Cohorts were reconstructed after the games with earlier feature clocks and are descriptive, not original pregame lists.

Every cell retains selected count, wins/losses, decided denominator, hit rate and unknowns. Exact category-by-cohort rows, source/position bands, coverage and uncertainty remain in [COHORT_VIEWS.csv](COHORT_VIEWS.csv). All prices and bookmaker settlements remain unavailable.
