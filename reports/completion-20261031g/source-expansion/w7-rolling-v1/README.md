## Rolling K timing and remaining limits

K6/10/20 applies per category, week and strategy with separate combined-side, Over-only and Under-only pools. At each increasing source-clock batch the rule ranks that batch by absolute saved point-line gap within category and uses remaining slots. It cannot revise earlier choices using later batches. The membership was frozen before labels. These are newly replayed rolling rules, not fixed weekly lists or original saved K recommendations. [Every K/category/actual-side result](ROLLING_K_VIEWS.csv).

Counts reconcile Over plus Under exactly. Hit rate is wins divided by wins plus losses; empty decided sides are N/A. Pushes, unknowns, missing actuals and abstentions remain separate. Bookmaker settlement remains unresolved even for statistically decided cases. One week cannot support a whole-week interval; game-cluster bootstrap sensitivity is exposed descriptive evidence without multiplicity adjustment. Different selected pools and coverage must be retained when comparing side rates.
