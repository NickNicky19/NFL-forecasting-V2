# Existing-source and retention scope

The timing wrapper preserves licensed nflverse/nflfastR public PBP/statistical derivatives (CC BY4.0) and their prior source/version bindings. It changes declared reconstruction clocks only; current revised values are not authenticated historical vintages. Existing FTN-derived controls retain their own prior CC BY-SA4.0 attribution and restrictions; no new FTN collection, annotation or fit occurs.

New models are trusted typed JSON derived from the permitted public football inputs. Restricted A/B offer rows, private archives and original protected models are not published. Public aggregates/hashes do not grant access to those rows. Owner-local identity, quote and settlement checks remain limited as recorded.

Bound resource scopes remain separate: source-policy derivation, historical model/prediction wave, existing score adapter and append-only registry/index metadata. All old failure receipts and consumed fit allocations remain. No private recovery gate or capture reserve is reset, no archive is deleted and no new shared off-GitHub store is created.
